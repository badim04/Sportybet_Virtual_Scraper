How to use this board. A bucket earns HOLDS only if both halves land on the same side of profit and the win rate moved less than 3 points. Chasing anything marked FLIPPED is how you lose money to randomness — it looked good in the first half and lied in the second.

If a bucket keeps showing HOLDS as the sample grows over the coming weeks, that's when it's worth turning into a real filter (via allowed_calls / single_odds_min/max in bettor_config.json) — not before.
